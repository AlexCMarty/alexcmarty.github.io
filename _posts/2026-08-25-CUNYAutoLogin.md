## What exactly is CUNY SSO?

All students at the City Univerity of New York have to use CUNY's Single Sign On (SSO). It logs you into
- CUNYFirst (accept financial aid, request official transcripts, apply for classes, etc.)
- Brightspace (submit assignments, see assignments, etc.)
- Degreeworks (see progress towards degree)
- etc

CUNY SSO is required to do basically anything at CUNY. 

## What's wrong with CUNY SSO?

The problem is that CUNY **signs you out every few hours**. Multiple times a day students must:

1. Enter their email & password
2. Pull out their phone and open their authenticator app
3. Enter the correct six digit code

I don't know about you, but I don't like doing the whole sign in thing several times a day. 

## How does CUNYAutoLogin solve this?

CUNYAutoLogin is a browser extension for both Chrome & Firefox. After installing the extension, it guides you through entering your email, your password, and adding a new 2FA factor from CUNYFirst, then securing your credentials with biometrics. Every time afterwards your sign in to CUNY SSO looks like this:

1. **Click** on something that redirects you, like Brightspace
2. **Wait**. Without clicking anything else, watch as it enters the information & clicks through for you 
3. **Enjoy** being signed in to CUNY

**One click. That's it**
